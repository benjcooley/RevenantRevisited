## Behavior and units

Ripple starts at scale .5, grows 1/16 per 24 Hz authored tick (1.5/sec). Atlas phase advances every other authored tick:0..3 cyclic while frame≤length−24, then4..15 dissipate. Rendered atlas cell is `{3,2,1,0,7,6,5,4,11,10,9,8,15,14,13,12}[15−ripframe]`. Source kills the ring at frame>length and phase15. Continuous growth is interpolated; the atlas remains discrete.

Length>24 triggers 2 or 3 splash droplets at local (0,0,5), with vx/vy=random(-length,length)/32, vz=random(length/2,length)/20 in wu/tick. Gravity is .37wu/tick² =213.12wu/sec². Landing spawns a ripple length/2 at parent surfaceZ; those may in turn splash if length>24. All children retain their own elapsed ages.

Drip's defaults are ripple length 64, height 128, period 48. Dead-state wait increments time, then tests `time>period && !random(0,period/2)`. Zero period-half has one possible outcome and must not be changed to a 50% trial. Spawn sets localZ=height,vz=−1.85wu/tick; the alive integration runs in the same tick as respawn. Position uses the source's explicit Euler order, velocity decreases .37 after advancing position. Interpolating the discrete trajectory produces smooth motion without changing authored boundary positions. Source height 128 lands on tick 23 of flight, not the 21-tick claim in the inventory. Mean dead wait is period+period/2+1 ticks (73 ticks for 48), not period×(1+period/4).

The native sequence has a changing tiny ripple near screen `(320,280)`. The port's central effect stays static and its log has no landing/ripple-spawn events throughout eight seconds of warmup plus five seconds of recording. Thus this comparison is **not accepted**, even though a Drip component reaches submission.

The concrete cause is eager component registration in the water object builder. `TObjectClass::NewObject` assigns a temporary runtime map index and activates attached components; `TObjectInstance::Load` subsequently replaces it with the saved index. `TSafeComponentRef` in the update registry captures the temporary owner index, becomes stale, and is erased by `RunUpdateList`; `Activate` cannot re-register an already-active component. Water builders now construct only. The existing imagery-ready `AttachAnimatorComponents` hook attaches the reference component after full load, with stable identity and serialized parameters. This also covers runtime-created instances on their first drawable build; direct diagnostic factories retain their independent manual update path. A component-ready log records actual loaded parameters for the next map capture. The broader identity-change policy belongs to the shared component lifecycle and later effect-system migration.

The shipped scene also exposes missing colored TileLight illumination in the port. Read-only tracing found `sectorLightIntensity` treating `SLightDef.multiplier` as percent (`/100`), while the original `colortable.cpp` brightness helper explicitly uses `multiplier/(multiplierscale/2)` with `multiplierscale=20`, hence `/10`. Nearby torch multipliers10 become ten times too dim under the percent interpretation. Tile DLS color transfer separately uses `/20`, so a faithful shared 2D/3D lighting policy still needs evidence and validation. No lighting edits or arbitrary tuning were made by this water review.
